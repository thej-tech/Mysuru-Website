Welcome to Mysuru

A city where royal history, beautiful architecture, vibrant traditions and peaceful landscapes come together.

From the magnificent Mysore Palace to the hills of Chamundi, Mysuru offers a glimpse into the rich heritage of Karnataka.

A City With A Story

Mysuru, formerly known as Mysore, is one of the most culturally significant cities in Karnataka.

The city is famous for its royal history, grand palaces, traditional arts, silk, sweets and the world-famous Mysuru Dasara celebration.
