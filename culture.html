Culture, Craft and Celebration

Mysuru's identity is shaped by music, traditional crafts, regional food and public festivals. These living traditions connect the city's royal past with everyday life today.

Mysuru Dasara

Mysuru Dasara is a ten-day celebration held during Navaratri, with cultural performances, exhibitions and decorated city landmarks.

The festival's grand procession, known as the Jumboo Savari, is a celebrated highlight. The illuminated Mysore Palace is another memorable sight during the festivities.

Silk, Painting and Handcrafts

Mysore Silk

Mysore silk sarees are valued for their smooth fabric, vibrant colours and distinctive zari borders. Silk weaving remains an important part of the region's craft heritage.

Mysore Painting

This classical painting tradition is known for detailed lines, graceful figures and rich colours. Artists traditionally finish selected details with delicate gold foil.

Music and Dance

The city has a long association with Carnatic music, classical dance and performances supported by its historic royal court.

Flavours of Mysuru

Mysore pak is a much-loved local sweet made with gram flour, ghee and sugar. Its rich flavour and soft, crumbly texture have made it popular across Karnataka and beyond.

Visitors can also explore the city's food culture through its traditional South Indian breakfasts, filter coffee and the bustling stalls of Devaraja Market.
