Famous Places in Mysuru

Mysore Palace

The magnificent former royal residence is one of Mysuru's most iconic landmarks. Its Indo-Saracenic design blends Indian, Islamic, Gothic and neoclassical influences.

Learn More →

Chamundi Hill

Rising above the city, Chamundi Hill is an important spiritual and cultural landmark. At its summit stands the Chamundeshwari Temple, dedicated to the city's patron goddess.

Learn More →

Brindavan Gardens

A beautifully landscaped garden beside the Krishnaraja Sagara Dam, known for its terraces, fountains and evening illumination.

Learn More →

St. Philomena's Cathedral

A striking neo-Gothic cathedral and one of Mysuru's best-known architectural landmarks. Its tall twin spires and stained-glass windows make it a distinctive feature of the city.
